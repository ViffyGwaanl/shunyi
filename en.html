02Privacy

No account. No server. No analytics.

The developer never receives anything you translate, or any data about you.

  • It happens on your Mac

    Offline translation and text recognition use what's built into macOS. They run on your Mac and never touch the network.

  • It only goes where you send it

    Only if you add your own AI endpoint does the text you translate leave your Mac, and it goes straight to that endpoint, not through us.

  • History stays local

    Settings and your last 200 translations live in the app's own sandbox. Clear them any time; deleting the app removes them too.

03Getting started

Four things to check after installing

A settings window opens the first time you launch the app. Its Overview page lists four items; once the first two are done, you're ready.

  1. Accessibility Required

    Used to copy your selection at the moment you press the shortcut, and to paste the translation when you choose Replace. It does not log what you type.

  2. Offline language pack Recommended

    Download it once and translation works without a network. Pick your own language under General → Languages.

  3. Screen Recording Optional

    With it on, the screen freezes while you frame a region, which makes video subtitles easy to catch.

  4. AI translation Optional

    In Settings, open AI Translation and enter your own OpenAI-compatible endpoint and API key. Both Chat Completions and Responses are supported, and model thinking can be switched on or off.

04Questions

Stuck? Start here

Nothing happens when I press the shortcut

Accessibility is probably off. Open System Settings → Privacy & Security → Accessibility and turn on Page & Pause Translate. If it's already on, turn it off and on again.

Which languages does it support?

Twenty, in any direction: English, 简体中文, 繁體中文, 日本語, 한국어, Français, Deutsch, Español, Italiano, Português, Русский, العربية, ไทย, Tiếng Việt, Bahasa Indonesia, Türkçe, Polski, Nederlands, Українська and हिन्दी. The interface itself comes in English, 简体中文 and 繁體中文.

I only get the offline translation, never the AI one

AI translation needs your own endpoint and key. After entering them under AI Translation, click Test Translation to check the connection. If it says Responses isn't supported, switch the API type back to Chat Completions.

Ask AI opened the site but didn't send my question

Some sites don't accept a pre-filled question in the URL. In that case the question is put on your clipboard: click the input box and press ⌘V. The first time you use a site, sign in to it in your browser first.

I'd like to use a Siri Remote

Siri Remote buttons work through the third-party tool HyperVibe. In Settings, open Shortcuts, click “Allow Access to the HyperVibe Folder…” and choose ~/.config/siriremote. You can then assign actions to remote buttons from the menus.

How do I uninstall it?

Quit the app and drag it from Applications to the Trash. Its settings and history live in its own sandbox container and are removed with it.
